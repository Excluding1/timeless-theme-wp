# Backlink strategy — Timeless Resurfacing (2026-07-08)

## What a backlink is (plain English)
A **backlink** is a link on **someone else's website** that points to yours. Google treats each
one as a vote: "this site is worth linking to." Along with content and reviews, backlinks are one
of the top ranking factors — especially for beating older competitors (Bathroom Werx etc.) who
have a 20–30 year head start on links. Reviews + citations + backlinks are exactly the three
things the SEO audit said we're behind on (our on-page is already better than everyone above us).

**Dofollow vs nofollow:** a "dofollow" link passes ranking power (link equity). A "nofollow" link
(most directories + social) doesn't pass equity but still helps — traffic, brand signals, and
Google uses it as a hint. A natural, safe profile has BOTH. Don't chase only dofollow.

## The golden rule (what NOT to do — these get you penalised)
NO buying links, NO PBNs (private link networks), NO link farms / "500 backlinks for $50" gigs,
NO blog-comment spam, NO hidden links. Google's Penguin/SpamBrain catches these and it can TANK a
young domain. Every link below is earned or legitimately claimed. Slow and real beats fast and fake.

## The plan — 5 tiers, easiest first (target ~3–5 new links/week)

### Tier 1 — Citations / directory listings (do first, ~3/week)
These are NAP (Name, Address, Phone) listings. Many are nofollow but they drive the LOCAL PACK
(the map results) via consistency, and several are dofollow. Use the identical business details
every time (see `docs/templates/citations-nap-pack.md`). The 15 to claim:
Google Business Profile (already have) · Bing Places · Apple Business Connect · TrueLocal ·
Yellow Pages AU · Yelp AU · Word of Mouth · StartLocal · Localsearch · Hotfrog · dLook ·
Aussie Web · Australian Business Directory · Brownbook · Cylex.
Plus the trade platforms (these ALSO rank and send leads): **hipages, Oneflare, ServiceSeeking,
Airtasker, ProductReview.com.au** — claim a profile even if you don't pay for leads; the profile
link + reviews there help.

### Tier 2 — Industry & supplier links (high relevance, ~1 week each)
- **Hawk / Australasian Resurfacing Supplies** (our coating supplier) — ask to be listed on their
  "find an applicator / where to buy" or installer page. Supplier links are gold for relevance.
- Any tool/product brand we use with a "find a pro" directory.
- Trade associations: Master Painters Association NSW, HIA (Housing Industry Association),
  relevant restoration/regrouting bodies — membership usually includes a member-directory link.
- ASbestos-awareness / SafeWork training provider — if they list certified businesses.

### Tier 3 — Local & community links (powerful for Sydney relevance)
- **Sponsor a local junior sports team / school fete / community event** (~$100–300) → nearly
  always gets a "our sponsors" link with a Sydney address = strong local signal + goodwill.
- Local chamber of commerce / business network membership → member link.
- Local Facebook community groups, "recommend a tradie" threads (participate genuinely, don't spam).
- Bunnings/hardware community boards, local council small-business listings.

### Tier 4 — Partner / referral links (also grows the business)
The people who refer us jobs can also link us — reciprocal and natural:
- **Plumbers, real estate agents, property managers, renovators, strata managers** — a "trusted
  trades we work with" link on their site. Offer the same back. Ties directly to the PM nurture
  play (the AitkenRE relationship + Post 7 the PM blog).
- Interior designers / home stagers who need surfaces refreshed before sale.

### Tier 5 — Content-driven / digital PR (slowest, highest value)
Our 8 blog posts are **linkable assets** — honest guides other sites want to reference:
- Pitch the "Resurface or Replace" + "Regrout or Retile" guides to Sydney reno blogs, local news
  "home improvement" columns, and real-estate content sites as an expert resource.
- HARO-style: answer journalist requests (SourceBottle is the AU version) as a "Sydney resurfacing
  expert" → earns news-site links.
- Before/after case studies (the Claremont Meadows job) pitched to local-interest outlets.
- Guest post on a complementary (not competitor) site: a plumber's or renovator's blog.

## Sequence (first 90 days)
- **Weeks 1–4:** Tier 1 (all 15 citations + 5 trade platforms) + GBP fully built. This is the base.
- **Weeks 3–8:** Tier 2 (supplier + associations) in parallel.
- **Weeks 6–12:** Tier 3 (one local sponsorship) + Tier 4 (ask 3–5 partners).
- **Ongoing:** Tier 5 as the blog gets published + we have case studies.

## How to know it's working
- Free: **Google Search Console** → Links report (shows who links to you). This is another reason
  GSC is the #1 setup task.
- Free/cheap: Ahrefs Webmaster Tools (free for your own site) or a monthly Ubersuggest/Ahrefs-lite
  to track referring domains climbing.
- The metric that matters: **number of referring DOMAINS** (unique sites), not raw link count.
  Ten links from ten sites beat 100 links from one.

## Reality check
Backlinks are a compounding, months-long play — not a switch. But we start from near-zero, so the
first 20–30 real referring domains (very achievable via Tiers 1–4 in 90 days) move the needle a lot
on a young domain. Pair this with the reviews push (7 → 20+) and the blog, and that's the honest
route from page 2/3 to page 1, then the local pack.
