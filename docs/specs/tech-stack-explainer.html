Who does what (today)

Your model copies Surface Care: the office runs ads + comms + quoting + dispatch + money; the sub just shows up and does the physical work. Here's the split right now, before AI agents or hires.

Allan

  • All customer comms (SMS, email, calls), your voice
  • Review photos + write the quote
  • Google Ads + landing pages
  • Send quote, take the accept
  • Approve big decisions
  • Pay the sub (Westpac)

Marko

  • Recruit + onboard subs
  • Dispatch the job in ServiceM8
  • QC before/after photos
  • Handle sub questions (not customer)
  • Does first jobs himself (Phase 1)

Automated

  • Form → contact + opportunity (W1)
  • Slack pings + customer SMS acks
  • Quote follow-up cadence (W3)
  • Deposit-paid → create SM8 job (Make)
  • SM8 complete → GHL + Accounts (Make)
  • Invoice reminders, NPS request

AI-assist (soon)

  • Quote-drafting agent: reads photos + form, drafts the quote you approve
  • Hot-lead auto-scoring
  • Draft customer replies in your voice

Hire (later)

  • Quote prep / photo + data admin
  • Customer-service replies (your voice)
  • Dispatch coordination
  • Human quoters on a framework
The rule we locked (Decision 7): every customer-facing word is Allan-owned + Allan-voiced. Marko is invisible to the customer. Subs only do physical work. When AI or a hire takes over comms later, they still write as Allan, under your accountability, so the brand stays one voice. Jordan does the same: his sales team quotes 180+ jobs/mo from a codified framework, never having touched a grinder (transcript 2026-04-30:465).

The AI & hire roadmap (0 → 185 jobs/mo)

Principle: automate the predictable, AI-assist the judgement-heavy-but-repetitive, hire humans for the relationship + edge cases + the physical supply. Add each layer only when volume makes the previous one hurt.
