# The Two-Engine Plan — autopilot organic portfolio funding real SaaS (2026-07-11)

**Engine A:** a factory that ships complete, automated, organic-revenue sites (domain → production
→ full SEO → monetization → autopilot ops). Cash on the side, compounding.

the distribution work (sales/support) that autopilot sites avoid.

### Honest cadence — "10 a week" vs reality
10 sites/week = a thin-content farm. Google's scaled-content systems would suppress the lot, and
each site's real work (unique data ingestion + quality layer) is exactly what can't be rushed —
it's also the moat. The bottleneck isn't build speed, it's VALIDATED ideas: round 2 proved even
100 generated ideas yield only ~5-8 genuinely open, worth-building plays.

**Realistic factory cadence:**
- Weeks 1-2: Site #1 (CareInspect) — slow on purpose; builds the reusable template.
- Weeks 3+: **1 site/week sustainable** (template + validation pipeline both exist).
- Month ~3: **10 sites live**, staggered — a revenue ladder where each month a new site exits
  the Google sandbox as another enters it.
- Then STOP adding. Scale the top 3-5 (more cities/states/countries per winner beats new domains),
  kill the losers at their day-90 gates.

### Portfolio math (from our calibrated sims, staggered starts)
- 10 validated sites: expect ~3-4 killed at gates, ~4-5 at $1-5k/mo, ~1-2 above $5k/mo.
- Portfolio P50 ≈ **$15-30k/mo around month 18-24**; 36-month cumulative profit P50 ≈ $300-700k.
- Total running cost for all 10: **~$100-200/mo** + ~$150/yr domains. Effectively all margin.
- Kill gates per site: day 30 (indexed?), day 60 (impressions growing?), day 90 (first $ / clear
  trajectory?) — fail → 301 anything useful to a sibling, stop investing.

### Country expansion (the "worldwide" answer)
Each winner replicates per country (US → AU → UK → CA), reusing the codebase with a new data
ingest. AU versions get Allan's domain expertise (trades, rentals, aged care). Expansion of a
proven winner ALWAYS beats a new unproven domain.

## ENGINE B — proper SaaS / AI products

**Trigger:** Engine A clearing ~$5-10k/mo consistently (≈ month 9-15). Its cash funds what
autopilot sites can't buy: paid acquisition experiments, a part-time human for sales/support,
premium data sources.

**Selection discipline (same rigor, different constraints):** dedicated Foundry rounds themed on
recurring-pain, high-WTP, self-serve-first SaaS. Accept founder-led sales for the first 20
customers. Kill criteria: 5 paying design partners in 60 days or reshape.

**The bridge:** Engine A sites ARE Engine B's distribution — audiences, email lists, domain
authority, and embedded Phase-2 SaaS tiers (CareInspect operator dashboard, Permitly contractor
workspace, TradeSchoolROI school analytics) that are literally SaaS products born with free
distribution. The best Engine B products will likely GRADUATE from an Engine A site's data.

**Economics:** SaaS exits at 3-5× ARR vs content at 2-3× profit — Engine B is where the
$1M+ asset value gets built; Engine A is what pays for the attempts without external funding.
