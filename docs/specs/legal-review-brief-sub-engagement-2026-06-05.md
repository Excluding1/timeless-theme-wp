## 1. What we need from you
A written opinion on whether our subcontractor engagement model is correctly characterised as **genuine independent contracting** — not employment, not sham contracting — under the post-August-2024 Fair Work Act (Closing Loopholes; s15AA whole-of-relationship test), plus **redlines** to our subcontractor agreement to close any gaps.

**Please confirm or correct, specifically:**
1. The model rests on **substance** — own ABN, own ≥$10M public-liability insurance, own tools/materials/vehicle, commercial risk (the contractor rectifies their own defective work at their own cost), penalty-free right to refuse any job, genuine right to delegate, no exclusivity, per-job invoicing — **not** on how our dispatch app is configured.
2. The agreement carries **zero** PAYG / wage / leave / timesheet / roster language.
3. The posture **survives the March-2026 ATO + Fair Work Ombudsman sham-contracting clampdown** specifically targeting the building & construction industry.
4. Our dispatch app is **not** a "digital labour platform" under **Part 3A** of the Fair Work Act — Timeless is the **principal/customer** engaging subcontractors directly, **not** an intermediary connecting subcontractors to the public.

## 2. Who we are
Sydney (NSW) bathroom **resurfacing & shower regrouting** business; two-partner partnership; pre-revenue / launching. We sell jobs to residential (occasionally strata) customers and intend to engage NSW **subcontractors** to perform the work.

## 3. The engagement model (the facts relevant to classification)
- Subcontractors are **offered** jobs — never assigned. A sub logs into **Timeless's single-account dispatch app**, which reads our **one** ServiceM8 account through our backend (the sub never holds our credentials).
- The app has a **built-in accept/decline** that is **logged** (`audit_log` records every decline) — this is the contractor's **right of refusal**, and we can **evidence to an auditor** that declines are **penalty-free** (no counter, score, throttle, or coaching trigger).
- Each subcontractor runs **their own business**: own ABN, own **≥$10M** public-liability insurance, own tools/materials/vehicle, bears rectification cost, sets their own booking within the customer's window, may **delegate** to a suitably qualified + insured substitute, is free to work for others, and **invoices Timeless per job**.
- We do **not** roster, do **not** pay for time, run **no** timesheets, and do **not** hold subcontractors out as Timeless staff (no "our team"/uniform/company-email identity). Subcontractors receive the customer's **name + address only** — Timeless owns all customer communication.
- The app is a **convenience** for offering + completing work; it is explicitly **not** the basis of the relationship and confers no employment.

## 4. The canonical statement we use (please pressure-test it)
> Timeless engages each subcontractor as a genuine independent contractor. Work is **offered**, never assigned: the contractor receives job *offers* through Timeless's dispatch app and **accepts or declines each one at their sole discretion, with no penalty, ever, for declining**. Each contractor operates **their own business** — own ABN, own ≥$10M public-liability insurance, own tools/materials/vehicle, own commercial risk (rectifies substandard work at own cost) — sets their own booking within the customer's window, may delegate to a suitably qualified + insured substitute, is free to work for others, and **invoices Timeless per job**. The dispatch app is a convenience for offering and completing work; it is **not** the basis of the relationship and confers no employment. It is **not** a digital labour platform under Part 3A — Timeless is the principal/customer, not an intermediary. The contractor is **not** rostered, **not** paid for time, **not** on a timesheet/shift schedule, and is **not** held out as Timeless staff.

## 5. Guardrails we have built in (for your review)

**App (A1–A9):**
- A1 — offer-language only (never "assigned/rostered/your shift")
- A2 — no auto-assign (offer → decline → re-route, never re-assign)
- A3 — decline is silent + zero-penalty (no counter/score/throttle/coaching trigger; reason optional)
- A4 — no roster/timesheet/clock-in UI (geofence may log job *duration* for ops, never as worked hours)
- A5 — no "our team"/uniform/company-email identity for subs
- A6 — a real delegation / hand-back path
- A7 — compliance gate (ABN present + PL verified + not expired) before any offer
- A8 — per-job subcontractor **invoice** (not an app-generated payslip)
- A9 — customer **name + address only** to the sub

**Agreement (B1–B11):**
- B1 — independent contractor (no PAYG/wage/leave language)
- B2 — ABN listed
- B3 — PL ≥$10M annual certificate
- B4 — own tools + materials
- B5 — per-job pay against the sub's invoice (no timesheet)
- B6 — express penalty-free right to refuse (no "must accept all")
- B7 — no exclusivity
- B8 — genuine delegation right
- B9 — sub bears rectification cost
- B10 — mutual 14-day termination
- B11 — rests on **substance** (own business/tools/ABN/risk), **not** "the app proves independence"
