## THE PROCESS — start to finish

### Phase 0 · Gather (5 min)
- Your **phone** (for the myID app)
- **Two Australian ID docs**: any 2 of passport / driver licence / Medicare card / birth certificate
- Timeless's **ABN**
- Your **personal TFN** + Marko's (for the personal returns — NOT the partnership TFN, which we'll find in Phase 3)
- This sheet + the bank statement + supplier invoices saved somewhere

### Phase 1 · Set up **myID** (10 min) — your personal digital key
1. App Store / Google Play → search **"myID"** (formerly myGovID — blue fingerprint logo, by ATO). Install.
2. Open → enter your email → verify the code.
3. Add your **two ID documents** → this gets you to **"Standard"** identity strength (that's all RAM needs).
4. Done. myID is now your login for government business services.

### Phase 2 · Link to the business in **RAM** (5 min)
1. On a computer, go to **info.authorisation.gov.au** (Relationship Authorisation Manager).
2. Click **"Login with myID"** → it shows a 4-digit code → type it into your myID app to approve.
3. As a **partner on the ABN**, you're the **"principal authority"** → RAM finds Timeless's ABN → **link it**.
   - *If it doesn't auto-find the business:* the ABN's associate/contact details with the ATO may need updating, or it may post a code to the business address to confirm. Follow its prompts.
4. *(Optional)* Authorise **Marko** as an authorised user so he has access too.

### Phase 3 · **ATO Online services for business** — find TFN + lodge the BAS (15 min)
1. Go to **onlineservices.ato.gov.au/business** → **Login with myID**.
2. **Grab the partnership TFN first:** it's shown under the business **Profile / business details** — write it on this sheet. *(This answers your "where's the TFN" question.)*
3. **Lodge the Q4 BAS:**
   - Menu → **Lodgments → Activity statements** → open the **Apr–Jun 2026** statement.
   - Enter **G1 = 1920**, **1A = 174.55**, **1B = 174.35**.
   - It calculates net ≈ **$0.20 payable**.
   - **Submit** → save the **receipt/confirmation number**.
   - **Pay** the tiny amount via BPAY/card using the PRN shown (or, if 1B ends up slightly higher, you'll get a few-dollar refund to the business account instead).
4. ✅ BAS done — the urgent one is handled.

### Phase 4 · Partnership tax return (30 min) · not due till 31 Oct — do after the BAS
The partnership return has **no myTax wizard**, so self-lodge one of two free ways:
- **A) Paper form (simplest for numbers this small):** on ato.gov.au search **"Partnership tax return 2026"** (NAT 0659) → download/order it → fill it in by hand using the **Section 2** numbers above → **mail** it to the ATO address printed on the form.
- **B) SBR-enabled software** if you'd rather do it on-screen.
- Needs the **partnership TFN** (from Phase 3) + **ABN**. Shows total income $1,845.45, the three expense lines, net profit $101.86, and the 50/50 distribution.

### Phase 5 · Personal returns via **myTax** (15 min each) · due 31 Oct
*This is a DIFFERENT login from the business portal — it's your personal myGov.*
1. Go to **my.gov.au** → link to the **ATO** → open **myTax** → start the **2026** return.
2. Most of it **prefills** (any wages, bank interest).
3. Add **Item 13 "Partnerships and trusts"** → distribution **$50.93**.
4. Review → **Submit**. Save the receipt.
5. **Marko repeats** the same for his **$50.93** on his own myGov.

### Phase 6 · Wrap up
- Pay the (near-nil) BAS; note every receipt number.
- **Keep all records 5 years** (statement + invoices) — that backs up your $174 in GST credits.
